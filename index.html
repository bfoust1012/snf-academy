Understand Your Buyers

AH

The Licensed Administrator (LNHA)

The Executive Decision Maker

Primary Driver: Facility-wide profitability, protecting the referral pipeline, managing state survey risks, and maintaining high occupancy rates.

Daily Stresses: Nursing labor shortages, escalating agency staff spending, and maintaining their public Five-Star Quality Star Rating.

How to Pitch: Frame your product around ROI. Show them how your tool reduces nurse turnover, keeps agency staff hours low, or protects Medicare revenue.
DN

The Director of Nursing (DON)

The Clinical Shield

Primary Driver: High-quality clinical outcomes, patient safety, nurse retention, and preparing for strict state surveys.

Daily Stresses: Staff burnout, floor nurses taking too long to chart in the EMR, medication errors, and hospital readmissions.

How to Pitch: Never pitch a tool that adds work. Instead, pitch how your product **reduces screens and clicks**, saving hours of charting time for their busy nurses.
MC

The MDS Coordinator

The Financial Auditor

Primary Driver: Complete documentation accuracy, meeting strict assessment dates (ARDs), and avoiding billing audits.

Daily Stresses: Chasing down floor nurses for incomplete ADL records, diagnosing coding errors, and backlogged paperwork.

How to Pitch: Pitch your tool as a safety net. Explain how it automatically pulls documentation into the EMR, saving them hours of tedious auditing before clinical submissions.
